About

About the Platform

Website Uptime and SSL Monitor checks whether a website is reachable, measures how long it takes to respond, and reads the website's SSL certificate to find out when it expires. Every check is saved so you can see the history and the uptime percentage over time.

Why monitoring matters

A website that is down loses visitors and trust, and often the owner is the last to know. A slow website frustrates users. An expired SSL certificate makes browsers show a security warning that stops most visitors from continuing. Regular checks help you find these problems early.

Key Features

Uptime Monitoring: Check whether websites are reachable or unavailable.

Response Time Tracking: Measure how quickly websites respond.

SSL Certificate Monitoring: Check SSL certificate validity and expiry information.

Monitoring History & Analytics: Review previous checks, response-time trends, and uptime statistics.
